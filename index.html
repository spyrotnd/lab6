Play Valorant with your campus squad

More ways to level up

Duelist Practice

Learn how to take first contact and open up a site for your team.

Details
We practise entry timing with agents like Jett, Reyna and Phoenix. You will learn when to push and when to wait for your team's utility.

Duelist Practice

Duelist practice runs every Monday from 6 to 8 pm in the UTM esports lab.

Initiator Utility

Find enemies before your team walks in. Info wins rounds.

Details
We go over recon and flash timing with agents like Sova, Skye and Fade, so your duelist always has the info they need.

Initiator Utility

Initiator sessions are on Tuesdays from 6 to 8 pm. Bring headphones so you can hear the callouts.

Controller Smokes

Block sightlines and control the pace of every round.

Details
We learn the standard smoke spots on Ascent, Bind and Haven with agents like Omen, Brimstone and Astra.

Controller Smokes

Controller practice is on Wednesdays from 6 to 8 pm. New players are welcome.

Sentinel Setups

Lock down a site and watch your team's back.

Details
We share simple trap and wall setups for Killjoy, Cypher and Sage that are easy to learn and hard to get past.

Sentinel Setups

Sentinel sessions are on Thursdays from 6 to 8 pm in the esports lab.

Aim Training Nights

Warm up together and build better crosshair placement.

Details
We start in the Range, then play Deathmatch as a group. Volunteers can help you pick a sensitivity that feels right.

Aim Training Nights

Aim nights are every Friday from 5 to 7 pm. All ranks, from Iron to Radiant, can join.

Team Tryouts

Want to compete? Try out for our UTM tournament team.

Details
Tryouts include two scrim matches and a short chat about teamwork. We look at communication as much as aim.

Team Tryouts

Tryouts are on Online!
